This first pass: one page that explains the cell as the article does (architecture, the four flows, the five altitudes, the build order), with a clear "not built yet" status, links to the article, riskmandate.ai and agenticcells.site. No screenshots of real mail; no person or company named beyond RiskMandate, AgentiCCells, SGit and Google; nothing invented.
