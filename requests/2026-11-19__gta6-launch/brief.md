## Topic rules (strict)
- Say plainly that the site is unofficial and not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Take-Two, Sony or Microsoft. Use names only to describe; never imitate a brand.
- No logos, game art, trailer frames, screenshots or other third-party images or video. Our own simple graphics or none.
- Every fact gets a source (official first) and a "checked on" date. Mark rumours and leaks as rumours, or leave them out. Never present a guess as a fact. If facts change, update the page or say it is out of date.
- Name no private person. Public figures only as their public role requires.
- No tragedies, disasters, crimes, health scares, elections or political campaigns, and no topic that profits from fear or grief.
- No tracking, analytics, ads, cookies or third-party scripts. Everything is static.
- No affiliate links, no selling, no sign-ups, no contact forms. Never contact anyone, never post to social media.
- No more than 3 new sites in any 24 hours until cells.b@ or Dinis says otherwise.
