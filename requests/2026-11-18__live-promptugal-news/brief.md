## Rules

- Stream, never record: the site shows live views only, through each operator's own public player or officially shared stream; no recording, no stored frames or clips, no re-streaming through our servers.
- Only public webcams that their owners have officially released or shared; never police, traffic-enforcement or private CCTV. Each camera listed with its operator, page URL, terms (whether embedding is allowed) and the date checked.
- Public, as every repository the factory creates. No secrets in the repository.
- Nothing about its content is invented; plain British English, no hype words, no em dashes.
