## 1. Black Friday: your rights when you buy (picked)
- What people ask: can I return something I bought in a Black Friday sale; how long do I have; do I get delivery back; what if it is faulty; does paying by credit card help.
- Official sources, all fetched on 2026-11-20:
  - GOV.UK "Accepting returns and giving refunds: the law" (https://www.gov.uk/accepting-returns-and-giving-refunds). It covers the same rights in sales, 14 days to cancel online orders, 14 more to return, refund within 14 days, the exceptions, repairs within 6 months, and 6 years to claim (5 in Scotland).
  - legislation.gov.uk:
    - Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, reg. 30
    - Consumer Rights Act 2015, s. 22 (30-day right to reject)
    - Consumer Credit Act 1974, s. 75 (£100 to £30,000)
  - GOV.UK "Consumer rights" (https://www.gov.uk/consumer-protection-rights), for who to contact in each nation.
  - US OPM federal holidays: Thanksgiving is on Thursday 26 November 2026, so Black Friday is on 27 November.
- Why our page helps: search results for this are mostly shops and deal sites. A plain page of the rules with the law linked helps people before and after they buy.
- Care needed: no deals, no shop names, no links to shops. It is general information, not legal advice; say so. UK only; say where Scotland differs.
