## What the site is for
Black Friday is on 27 November 2026, the day after the US Thanksgiving holiday (Thursday 26 November 2026, per the US Office of Personnel Management). Many people in the UK buy online in those sales and then ask what they can send back. One quiet page sets out the rules from GOV.UK and the legislation itself:
- the 14-day right to cancel online orders, and what is excluded;
- the 30-day right to reject faulty goods;
- repairs and replacements;
- the same rights in sales;
- credit card protection under section 75;
- where to get help in each nation.

## Topic rules (strict)
- Say plainly that the site is unofficial and not affiliated with any government body, retailer or card provider. It is general information, not legal advice.
- No logos or third-party images. Our own simple graphics or none.
- Every fact gets a source (official first) and a "checked on" date. If facts change, update the page or say it is out of date.
- Name no private person. No shop names, no deals, no links to shops.
