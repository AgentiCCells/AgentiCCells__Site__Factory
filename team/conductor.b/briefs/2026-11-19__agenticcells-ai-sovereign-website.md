## The one idea
"A website you own, control and can take anywhere." Sovereignty is not where a site is hosted; it is being able to move the site, its data and its schema. The platforms (Wix, Squarespace, Fliplet and the AI builders) cannot say this by their own terms; the website cell can.

## The six promises (from "The pitch"), led by sovereignty
1. You own it: a GitHub repository in your name, full history; yours within three months at the latest.
2. You control it: no platform, no subscription, open code nobody can withdraw.
3. You change it: by talking to your own Claude, with guard rails, every change undoable.
4. You can run it anywhere: static files on any host; GitHub Pages for free.
5. It looks better: five designs by a team of agents; you choose.
6. Almost nothing to attack, and it costs once: no server code, no database, no cookies by default.
